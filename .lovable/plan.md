# Technologics portfolio website

## What will be built
- A polished one-page studio portfolio for Technologics with clear navigation between the requested sections.
- A strong opening area with the studio name, scaling-focused positioning, and a contact call-to-action.
- Scannable services, technology stack, selected work, industry experience, studio overview, reasons to choose Technologics, and contact details.
- Portfolio presentation for Fameo with a “Launching Soon” status plus five discreet e-commerce project concepts that do not imply named clients.
- A professional contact form interface and footer with social placeholders.

## Visual direction
- Deep navy and slate foundation with a single cyan accent, restrained borders, crisp typography, and subtle motion.
- Dense but calm corporate layout designed for business clients in the Middle East and Europe.
- RTL-ready spacing and grid behavior, with mobile and desktop layouts that remain easy to scan.
- Purpose-made abstract product visuals for the portfolio rather than stock photography or client-identifying imagery.

## Technical details
- Define all colors, fonts, shadows, and motion centrally in the site design system.
- Keep the experience on the home page with accessible in-page navigation and semantic section structure.
- Add complete page metadata for search and social previews.
- Keep contact details explicitly labeled as placeholders where the brief does not provide final information; form submission will be a polished front-end interaction only because no delivery service was requested.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, form interaction, text fit, and console errors.
